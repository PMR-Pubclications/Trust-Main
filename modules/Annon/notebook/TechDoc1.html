Trust-Main & Trust-Shell

An App-Store-Free, Offline-Resilient Forensic & Dispatch Ecosystem with Secure Mobile Shelling & Blockchain Auditing
GitHub Repository ↗ Technical White Paper ↗ Transparency Portal ↗

The Motivation

For law enforcement, emergency medical services, and municipal hospitals, traditional software deployment is plagued by rigid app store restrictions, third-party telemetry risks, and severe connectivity bottlenecks in the field.

We built Trust-Main and Trust-Shell to solve this: an independent, high-security, open-source forensic and administrative ecosystem designed specifically for first responders. It operates completely free of commercial app stores, separating a lightweight client-side security wrapper (Trust-Shell) from a robust server-hosted data engine (Trust-Main), anchored by a blockchain-backed audit ledger.

Core Architectural Breakdown

1. Trust-Shell Access Layer

Acts as the lightweight, secure mobile client wrapper and authentication gate on department tablets, tunneling securely to primary data repositories hosted on the department's Trust-Main server backend.

2. Blockchain Audit Ledger

The ReportPackagePublisher commits cryptographic SHA-256 hashes of finalized forensic reports directly to an immutable, chained audit ledger complying with ISO/IEC 17025 standards.

3. App-Store-Free PWA

Bypasses commercial app store bottlenecks by installing instantly via Progressive Web App standards with standalone display
